# Sounds and music in /play

Every file here is CC0 (public domain). Thank you to the authors.
Rebuild with `tools/audio/fetch.sh && python3 tools/audio/build.py` (sources: tools/audio/manifest.json).

## Sound effects (audio/sfx)

- **Impact Sounds** by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 — https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- **RPG Audio** by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 — https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- **Interface Sounds** by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 — https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- **UI Audio** by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 — https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio

Spell, swing and ambience sounds that are not in these packs are made in the browser (audio.js).
