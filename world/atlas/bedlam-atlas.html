Bedlam World Atlas

Every room from Bedlam's own Mudlet map, saved 13 Nov 2013. Bedlam grew out of AnotherWorld, and the builder names after each zone come from the map file.

Source: bedlam-map-11-13-2013.dat, archived by the Wayback Machine from bedlam.mudportal.com. Terrain colors follow the room sector codes. Layout and coordinates are Bedlam's, as the mapper drew them in 2013.
