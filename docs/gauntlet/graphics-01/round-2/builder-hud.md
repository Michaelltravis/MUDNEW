# graphics-01 / round 2 (HUD pass 4) — builder: HUD and feed

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer.html` (one appended CSS block, last in the cascade; markup: `#combat-log` moved out of `#side-col`, new `#side-toggle` button), `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui.js` (drawer toggle, combat body class, ambient-prose filter, minimap sizing, ticker fold, row copy), `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui-arpg.js` (encounter-line hold 3400 -> 2600 ms).

- The right column is gone from the resting frame. Sector map / contacts / quest / first-visit prose live in a Tab-toggled drawer (`body.side-open`, also the "PANELS Tab" button bottom-right, Esc closes). The drawer's children are `visibility:hidden` with visible descendants because the scene reads the rects of `#minimap-wrap` / `#contacts` / `#quest-tracker` for play-area insets: it skips a hidden box, so opening the drawer overlays the world instead of re-zooming it (verified: camera stays 1256x660 @ 2.75 with the drawer open).
- Vertical chrome is one 48px strip and no top bar: the scene zoom-fits the 24x15 room in quarter steps, and a 672px canvas -> 660px viewport reaches zoom 2.75, so the room is 1056x660 of 1280x720 (82% of the width, 92% of the height; round 1 was 960x600 at 2.5 with a 280px column). `#hud` and `#compass` use the same hidden-box/visible-children trick so they can sit on the strip / in the right-hand void without becoming 350px side insets.
- Room title is a single translucent pill at the top edge (16px name, 11.5px zone) and hides in combat (`body.in-combat`, set from `combat.state` in ui.js) so the target frame is the only thing at the top of a fight; "IN COMBAT" moved beside the frame instead of over it.
- Dock, left: one big HP bar (22px, 13.5px numbers) with MP/MV as 98px bars beside it (12px numbers), under them a 12px `Lv 30 Warrior · 0% xp · 2075 gold` row and the three stance chips at 12px (icons dropped, labels kept). Crest/portrait removed (name+class+level merged into the row). Action bar labels 12px, key numbers 11px, proficiency % dropped from the slots (it stays in the tooltip); belt keys 10/11px.
- Message feed is a centred 3-line ticker on the world's bottom edge (`#combat-log` at bottom:68px, only the last 3 lines display, 13.5px on translucent pills, hits carry the colour, each line enters with a 0.3s rise and fades out 6.5s after it lands; a folded ×N line restarts its cycle). No header, no scrollback panel, no pointer events; the terminal (~) still keeps history.
- Nothing textual floats over a fight: ui.js wraps `MH.bus.emit` to drop `ambient.candidate` while `body.in-combat` (the scene bubbled short server lines naming the mob, e.g. "You swing at grave keeper... miss!", over the mob) and drops arrival/departure lines at all times (the scene already draws an in-world "-> south" cue).
- Combat stack re-based on the bare top edge: target frame at 8px (17px name, 12px tag, 12.5px HP text), wind-up bar at 84px, Q/E/X chips at 124px (13px); encounter line under them at 170px; centre chips / cast bar above the ticker (140/186px). Minimap now sizes to the drawer width instead of the letterbox band.
- Checked with `tools/gauntlet/capture.js` at 1280x720 (city, combat, dungeon) plus a drawer-open shot: room 1056x660 at (112,6), strip 48px, only the title pill, sky chip, compass and ticker touch the world's edges; page errors: none.
