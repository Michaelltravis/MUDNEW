# graphics-01 / round 2 — builder: HUD and feed

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer.html`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui-arpg.js`

- Reclaimed world area: the canvas now starts under the 54px top bar and ends above a single 110px dock, and every persistent side panel (sector map, contacts, quest tracker, first-visit prose, message feed) moved into one right-hand column (`#side-col`, 320px). Because the camera zoom-fits the 24x15 room in quarter steps from the panel rects, this lands on zoom 2.25: the room is 864x540 of 1280x720 (67% width) versus 672x420 (52%) in round 1. The duplicate rune globe (`#aether-sigil`) is hidden because it cost a left inset.
- Vitals are one big readout in the dock's bottom-left: a 24px HP bar with 13.5px bold white numbers, MP/MV half-width at 13px beneath it, then level/gold row and stance chips; `ui-arpg.js` now tags `#hud` with `hp-hurt` (amber fill) and `hp-low` (ember pulse) from the same payload that fed the globe, so state reads from the bar itself.
- Feed typography: lines 12.5px near-white (`#dbe9f0`), your hits jade-bold, enemy hits red-bold on a faint red band, misses dimmed; timestamps are no longer printed (they moved to the line's tooltip in `ui.js`) so the message text is the first thing read. The per-exchange beat separator and xN fold from round 1 are kept.
- Contacts names 13px white with 10.5px level line, quest tracker objectives 12px, hotbar labels/keys 11px, vitals readout row 11px, minimap/contacts headers 10.5px: nothing persistent is below ~10px now.
- Combat stack is tight under the top bar and in reading order: `IN COMBAT` chip sits in the top bar left of the room name (it was covering the zone line), then target frame (16px name, 18px HP bar) at 62px, enemy telegraph bar at 136px, reaction chips (12px) at 178px. Nothing combat-related sits on the feed or the dock.
- Dock rework: hotbar 54px slots centred, command line 560px wide so it clears the vitals, potion belt and compass share the dock's right end, help button in the world band's left gutter; transient overlays (cast bar, "draws near" caption, buff bar, recovery/detail cards) were repositioned so they neither cover the column nor the top bar.
- `ui.js`: removed the feed's drag/saved-height wiring since the column sizes it (flex:1 with a 92px floor); the collapse/clear header tools still work.
- Verified with `tools/gauntlet/capture.js` at 1280x720 (city/combat/dungeon): panels do not overlap the room, the column and dock are the only chrome, and the target frame is the only element over the room top in combat.
