# Builder: actors (graphics-01, round 2)

Files: src/web_isometric/platformer/scene-topdown.js, fx-abilities.js, dcss.js (lpc.js untouched).

- Hostile silhouette cue: the doll contour for hostiles went from a deep red that read as black (0x6e0a10, strength 5) to a vivid red rim (0xe8322e, strength 9); DCSS creature art bakes a 2px solid-disc red contour instead of a 1px eight-corner one (`MH.dcss.outlined` takes a `thick` multiplier). Hostiles also get a crisp red ellipse ring at the feet (`aggroGfx`), drawn above the darkness layer and pulsing faster once the mob is fighting; the soft ember pool under them is larger and brighter, and hostiles carve a wider light pool out of dark rooms.
- Wind-up telegraph: the rear-back is stronger (lean 22 deg, 0.84x0.84 wide / 1.3x tall, lifted 6px via a new `pose.dy` that both the doll and creature-art sync honour) and its ring graphics moved from depth 4.7 (under the darkness layer, so invisible in the crypt) to 40.5. The same graphics now also draw a red reticle that closes on the PLAYER's feet over `resolve_in`, with four ticks and a fading beam of intent from attacker to target; when the wind-up resolves a white impact star lands on the player.
- Hit flash: the struck body's white fill is longer (130ms; second blink on heavy hits) and a new `MH.abilityFx.impact` (fx-abilities.js) pops a white four-point star + ring on the struck actor, used for hits you land, hits you take, and resolved wind-ups.
- In-combat pose: the player doll now loops the LPC slash animation and turns to face the target while fighting (before, the swing only showed for 300ms after an engage). NPC dolls derive their facing from the hidden sprite's walk row + flip (a right-walker used to show its face) and square up to the player while fighting; creature art flips toward the player while fighting.
- Actor scale/anchor: creature art is 2 tiles tall (1:1 pixels for 32px DCSS art, about the player's body height) and bosses 2.5 (was 2.1 / 3.2), anchored at the bottom edge on the contact shadow like the dolls, so the bear stands on the floor at the same tile scale as the player. Spawn slots in the first floor rows are walked down to the first walkable row that gives a tall body headroom, so nothing (and no name label) overlaps the top wall.
- Melee stance: the mob AI used to circle at 22px (on the hero's tile, so the fighters stacked and one hid the other); a fighting melee mob now takes and holds a position beside the player on the same row, one body-width out (32px), flipping sides occasionally or when its side is a wall, with a small shuffle. Casters keep the old 52px circling.
