# graphics-01 / round 1 — builder: atmosphere

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer/painter.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/themes-zones.js` (`immersion.js` unchanged this pass; its prose-card auto-lift from the previous pass stands).

What BrowserQuest still had over our last shots (reference vs `docs/gauntlet/graphics-01/round-1/mh/`): its village is houses, sand-vs-green ground and a lamp, ours was a brick box on grey cobbles; its clearings have a lit side and a shaded side, ours were one flat brightness edge to edge; our cobbles still showed a slab rhythm. The dry-01 critic's three fixes (biome floors, falloff in every zone, prose card off the map) were already in the tree; this pass takes the city label and the "uniform brightness" complaint.

- City walls are now painted as rooftops (`themes-zones.js` midgaard: `paintBorder`, `borderStyle: 'roof'`, six roof colours; `painter.js` step 6.5 `roofed` branch): each ~5-cell run of border is one house with its own terracotta/slate colour, two shingle courses per cell, a sunlit ridge, a dark eave over the street and a seam between neighbouring houses; the scene's existing dark lower strip becomes the house wall under the eave. Interior obstacle blocks get the same treatment as small outbuildings.
- Market / plaza / square rooms (matched on the room name) get a paved circle with rings and radial seams around the centrepiece, up to five striped market awnings leaning on the walls (never over an exit) with a counter, cast shadow, scalloped hem and a crate of goods in front; every cobbled open-sky room gets grass tufts where paving meets wall (corners almost always) and weedy patches, so the ground is sand-and-green like the reference rather than one brown.
- Cobble/flagstone brushwork: slab and seam alpha now vary per slab, seven big worn flagstones and nine packed-earth dust patches break the slab rhythm; stone rooms with exits get a wandering polished track with two dark cart ruts (the straight light cross is gone). Midgaard floor warmed to `#a08a66`.
- Light falloff outdoors: the sun gradient across the room is doubled (warm top-left 0.16, cool bottom-right 0.15), open-sky vignettes are stronger (`LIGHT_OUT` 0.16 -> 0.30 with a green-black colour, `LIGHT_SUN` 0.10 -> 0.22, painter fallbacks likewise) with the corner away from the sun sinking deepest, and every wall torch/lantern the scene hangs in an open-sky room now bakes a warm additive pool with real falloff under it (previously only enclosed rooms got pools).
- Forest: two or three broad sun shafts slant in from the upper-left (additive, soft-edged rotated bands), dapple pools are larger, and the ground within ~2.6 cells of a canopy block sits in deep green shade, so a clearing is brightest in the middle and darkens toward the treeline; floor pockets hemmed in by trees on three sides are painted as under-storey shade instead of showing as pale squares in the canopy.
- Shallows: four pale caustic rays cross the reef bed (same beam helper), giving the water an evident light source without touching the scene's swim wash. Dungeon and cave are unchanged beyond the shared code paths (both won round 1).
- Checked in headless captures of the five labels (scratch dir, `tools/gauntlet/capture.js`): all five arrive, the page error list is empty, QC passes. Known limits outside these files: the scene's faint cell-pitch grid line and per-cell wall-depth strips still stamp over the roofs; the room prose whisper can still drift over the map.
