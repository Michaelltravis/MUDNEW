# graphics-01 / round 1 — builder: atmosphere

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer/painter.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/themes-zones.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/immersion.js`.

What BrowserQuest does that ours did not (from the reference vs dry-01 shots): its room edges are masses (tree canopies, boulder ridges, reef) rather than one 16px tile repeated; its paths snake through the green; its ground colours are vivid and stay readable; the top third of our map was hidden behind the room-prose card.

- Organic borders are now painted INTO the room painting (`painter.js` step 6.5): treelines/hedges/pines as layered canopy lobes lit from the upper-left, cave walls as ridges of individual boulders with a sunward rim, dunes as sand ridges, reefs as pale rock with coral heads, fans and breaking-wave foam dabs, and a running-bond brick mass for dungeon/indoor sectors inside organic zones. Every lobe is jittered per room (vnum-seeded), so no stamp repeats and the outline is bumpy instead of a row of squares.
- `themes-zones.js` leaves the tile sprites for those kinds (`MH.PAINTED_BORDER_KINDS`: tree, pine, deadtree, hedge, rock, dune, coral) transparent when the painter is on, and `MH.SECTOR_ATMOS` now carries a `borderKind`/`borderCol` so a cave sector inside the forest zone paints rock walls (Bear's Den) instead of green tree tiles; straight-edged AO strips are replaced by round contact shadows under organic masses (the strips stepped along the bumpy outline).
- Paths on grass/sand/snow wander: a 5-point polyline with perpendicular wobble that dies at the exit, drawn as worn verge + road + trodden core with pebbles; grass gets sunlit sweeps and shaded hollows so the field has shape; cobbles gained tone/seam contrast; open-sky rooms get a straight cast-shadow band under every wall run in addition to the per-block sun shadow.
- Caves/mines: vignette 0.66 -> 0.78 with a near-black corner, torch pools larger (6.2 cells) with a hot additive core and a stronger warm tint pass, the central hearth pool dimmed (0.62 -> ~0.34) so the torches, not the floor middle, are the light; rock palette warmed (`#6a5442`) to survive the scene's blue cave grade.
- Shallows: the scene lays a flat 35% blue swim wash over the whole room (not editable here), so the shallows wash is pre-compensated: a near-white sandbar disc and shoals in the middle, over-bright cyan mid-water, a near-black rim, deeper water hugging every reef mass, additive caustics and glints; the sunken vignette drops 0.26 -> 0.14 and its mood wash 0.08 -> 0.04. Forest floor slightly more saturated (`#529440`).
- `immersion.js`: the first-visit room-prose card lifts after ~3.6 s (hovering it holds it), and immediately when the player presses a movement key or clicks the world; L still re-reads. This answers the critic's note that the card hid the top third of every map shot.
- Checked in headless captures of the five labels (scratch dir): the painter runs for all five, page errors list is empty, and QC passes. Known limits outside these files: a faint cell-pitch grid line and per-cell dark edge strips drawn by the scene still show over lit cave floor; the swim wash caps how sandy the reef centre can look.
- Not changed: dungeon (necropolis bone floor/walls) beyond the shared shadow band, since it won round 1.
