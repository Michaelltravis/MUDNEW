# graphics-01 / round 1 (HUD pass 3) — builder: HUD and feed

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer.html` (one appended CSS block, last in the cascade), `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui.js` (feed writer only). `ui-arpg.js` unchanged this pass (syntax-checked).

- Vertical chrome budget cut so the camera steps up a zoom notch: the scene zoom-fits the 24x15 room in quarter steps from the measured chrome rects, so a 40px top bar + 64px dock (was 54 + 110) leaves a 616px canvas -> 604px viewport -> zoom 2.5. The room is now 960x600 of 1280x720 (75% of the width, 83% of the height) versus 864x540 before; BrowserQuest's real edge over us was this vertical strip, not the side column.
- The right column narrows to 280px so it never forces the zoom back down (a 320px column would have capped the viewport at 936px). Contacts cap at 150px; the sector map scales to the column width.
- The dock is one 64px strip: 22px HP bar with 13.5px bold numbers, MP/MV half-width beneath, a 12px level/xp/gold row, and the three stance chips as a column beside the bars. The portrait (the crest already shows it), the XP hairline (the row says "x% xp"), the help button (H key, top-bar "?") and the "~ TERMINAL" tab (~ still opens it) leave the dock. `#hud` has a fixed 52px box because the scene treats any panel rising above the canvas bottom as a 350px left inset (the first capture shrank the room to 576x360 for exactly that reason).
- The command line is a transient: it is hidden until Enter focuses it, then floats above the dock and hides again on blur. The persistent "press Enter to type a command to the MUD..." placeholder was the only prose on the frame in every screenshot.
- The message feed hides when empty (`ui.js` strips `.show` at init and on clear) and room arrivals ("-> Market Square") are no longer echoed into it, so out of combat the feed does not exist and in combat it holds only hits, misses, telegraphs and speech. It anchors to the bottom of the column, caps at ~5 lines (148px) then scrolls, lines are 13px near-white, and lines older than 8s ease to 55% opacity (hover restores) so the newest exchange is the bright one.
- Top bar is a single 40px line: crest 32px, room name 17px with the zone line 10px under it, clock chip moved to the right edge (it was 218px in from the edge for no reason and clipped the zone text when the banner was made inline). Room hazard / context-action / momentum chips (`#center-stack`) now pack upward from the dock edge instead of hanging under the title, so the top edge has one focal point: the title when exploring, the target frame in combat.
- Combat stack re-based on the 40px bar: target frame at 46px, enemy wind-up at 120px, Q/E/X reaction chips at 160px; cast bar and "draws near" caption sit just above the dock.
- Checked with `tools/gauntlet/capture.js` at 1280x720 (city, combat, dungeon): room 960x600 at (15,45), column at x>=990, nothing over the room except the combat stack at its top edge and the transient chips at its bottom edge.
