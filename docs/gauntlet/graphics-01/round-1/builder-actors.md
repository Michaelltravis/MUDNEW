- Compared BrowserQuest city/combat/cave against our latest shots: the one thing theirs still did better on "find the player in one second" was that the hero is the ONLY figure of its kind on screen, while our Market Square had the hero and every cityguard drawn from the identical warrior loadout (great helm + crusader shield + grey plate), so the player was findable only by its blue ring.
- lpc.js `resolveLoadout` now distinguishes NPC dolls (they carry a seed; the player never does): NPCs of the warrior/paladin class drop the class-default shield and swap the great helm for the light cap, and every NPC torso without worn armour takes a seeded livery tint (brass / blued steel / bronze / verdigris / rose for guards, dyed cloth for townsfolk). The hero keeps the full kit as a unique silhouette; equipped NPC gear still wins over the defaults; the inventory portrait path uses the same loadout so it stays consistent.
- Wind-up telegraph stacking: the `!` mark sat on top of the name label and the `⚠ intent` banner overlapped the `!`, so mid-wind-up the mob wore a smear of text. The `!` now sits 12px above the label, the banner 36px, and the `⚔` fight mark hides while the `!` is up (restored when the wind-up ends).
- When a wind-up resolves into a strike, the enemy's own blow is drawn: a red-tinted slash arc sweeps across the player from the attacker's side (`slashFx` gained an optional tint), in addition to the existing forward lunge and white impact star — so the strike reads as their swing, not only as your flinch.
- Bystander personal space (scene wander AI): an idle NPC standing within 24px of the player steps aside immediately instead of waiting for its next wander tick, and wander targets within 30px of the player or 20px of another NPC are rejected; in the previous capture Guildmaster Brennan was standing inside the hero's silhouette.
- Verified with tools/gauntlet/capture.js (city, combat, cave; zero page errors): guards now read as bronze/steel-capped figures distinct from the shield-bearing hero; the hero's silhouette is not overlapped by a bystander.
