# playability-01 / round 3 — builder: feel (combat readability, feedback, rhythm)

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer/scene-topdown.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui-arpg.js` (`fx-abilities.js` untouched). Verified with a scratch copy of the capture flow that sends `zreset` before `kill keeper` (four captures, scratchpad only; no harness file changed).

- **Round 2 was never on camera.** Reproducing the capture without `zreset` gives the round-2 picture exactly (no keeper, 207/207, corpse label): the lead's fight transcripts kill the keeper minutes before `capture.js` runs. With `zreset` first, a fight appears in frames 0-3. Second harness note for the lead: at level 30 the keeper dies in 8-20 s of wall time and `page.screenshot()` takes ~6-7 s, so 4-6 of the 8 frames are post-kill whatever the client does; a tankier target, a lower `advance`, or `waitMs: 0` on the fight shot would put more of the exchange on film.
- **The ledger: the last number each side dealt stays on screen.** A hit number no longer vanishes after its 1 s pop — it parks beside its owner (your damage in the white/gold ladder over the foe's far side, the damage you took in red over yours) at full size until the next hit replaces it; cleared when combat ends, the foe's final number lingers 2.5 s over the body. A frame taken between two exchanges now still shows the score of the last one instead of depending on the shutter catching a pop. Numbers are also 3 pt larger across the ladder; word pops (STAGGERED!, SLAIN) are excluded from the bump and pop in at 1.3x instead of 1.9x, because a slow frame caught `STAGGERED!` mid-pop filling the arena. (`damageNumber`/`tickPops`/`parkPop`/`tickLedger`)
- **Hit flash on the wall clock, and a red wash instead of a pink blob.** `flashFill` restores via `setTimeout` (the scene-clock `delayedCall` ran seconds late at 1-3 fps and left a flat white/pink silhouette across whole frames). Taking a hit is now a 120 ms white blink then a multiplied red tint for ~0.3 s, so the sprite stays readable while obviously hurt. The struck mob also REELS (lean away + dip scaled by damage) on top of the squash, so "just got hit" is a pose as well as a flash. (`fxHit`, `fxTaken`)
- **Target bracket + wide HP bar on the engaged foe.** The foe you are fighting wears a four-corner gold bracket around its whole body (drawn above the sprites — at floor depth it was hidden behind two overlapping dolls) with a gold ring at its feet; its head HP bar goes from 18x2 to 32x3.5 px with a dark outline. Other hostiles keep the small red ellipse. (`update` engageRing block, `drawHpBar`)
- **Bodies stop stacking.** Melee stance ring 32 -> 40 px (two 0.7-scale dolls are ~36 px shoulder to shoulder, so at 32 the keeper stood half inside the knight); the "step to your side" unstack is re-armed every 2.5 s instead of once per fight, since a shove/lunge could stack them again mid-fight. (`update` mob AI)
- **Reaction prompt moved to the action bar, at button size.** `REACT NOW · [Q] BRACE · [E] SIDESTEP · [X] INTERRUPT` now sits just above the hotbar (bottom 138 px, over chrome not arena) at 12.5 px with a glowing lead badge, instead of a 9 px strip under the target frame at the top; the finisher chip moved up to 172 px so they never overlap. Hotbar ribbon text is 9 px, no-wrap, sits above the slot (`STRIKE NOW` was clipping to `NOW` inside a 56 px slot; it is `STRIKE!` now). (`ui.js` feel-css, `suggestSlot`)
- **Less prose over the fight.** The Dragon-Warrior "X draws near! Command?" typewriter box holds 1.8 s instead of 2.6 s (it was still over the lower arena two frames into the fight); the engage line "You attack X!" no longer pops a 'hit' number, and a hit without a parsed amount pops 'hit' but is never parked as the ledger (the ledger only ever shows a number). Per-round tell tint (no declared intent) is 520 ms on the wall clock instead of 330 ms on the scene clock. (`ui-arpg.js`, `fxHit`, `onCombatUpdate`)
