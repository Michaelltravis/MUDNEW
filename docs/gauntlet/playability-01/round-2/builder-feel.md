# playability-01 / round 2 — builder: feel (combat readability, feedback, rhythm)

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer/scene-topdown.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui-arpg.js` (fx-abilities.js untouched). Verified on a live keeper fight (zreset first) with a scratch copy of the capture flow; the round-1 storyboard had run against an already-dead keeper, so none of it was on camera.

- **Time was the bug, not the art.** In the headless capture the page renders at 1-3 fps and Phaser pins every frame's delta to 16.7 ms for 120 frames after any focus/resume and whenever the window is unfocused, so every tween ran 20-50x slow: a 0.85 s damage number hung for ten seconds, "STAGGERED!" filled the screen across four storyboard frames. `create()` now caps the panic cooldown at 8 frames and lets a slow frame count up to 1 s; `update()` re-asserts focus while the document is visible. (`scene-topdown.js`)
- **Damage numbers run on the wall clock.** `damageNumber` no longer uses tweens; a `_pops` list is stepped from `Date.now()` in `tickPops()` (pop-in, rise, fade, destroy), so a number lives 1 s of real time whatever the frame rate. Pop-in scale trimmed 2.4→1.9 so a crit never fills the arena. (`scene-topdown.js`)
- **Enemy stays beside you.** A fight that began with the mob on your tile hid one body in the other for the first frames; a fighting mob closer than 22 px is stepped straight to its stance one body-width beside you (once per fight), and both the mob stance step and your duel-dance velocity are capped at "reach it this frame" so a long frame no longer overshoots through the other body. (`scene-topdown.js`)
- **Last-beat tell + heavier landing.** At 82% of the wind-up the mob snaps solid white, the tell pill jumps a size and a white-hot ring flares at its feet ("about to hit NOW" is a different picture from "winding up"). On impact you are shoved 6-14 px by damage, the attacker lunges 14 px and squashes into the blow. (`startWindup`/`tickWindup`/`fxTaken`)
- **The kill is the loudest beat.** One-time (guarded against the double death path) gold + white shockwaves, a camera thud and a `SLAIN` stamp over the body; the screen-wash flash was tried and removed because it whited out the frame after the kill. (`fxMobDeath`)
- **Result line that stays.** `☠ YOU KILLED GRAVE KEEPER` plate (+xp folds in) above the feed, held like the reference's status bar until the next fight or room (settles smaller after 8 s, dropped after 60 s); the feed line became "☠ You killed grave keeper"; the typewriter's "Thou hast defeated" and the spurious "draws near" that fired 4 ms after a death are suppressed. (`ui.js`, `ui-arpg.js`)
- **Reactive action bar.** `MH.feel` tracks stagger / wind-up / foe HP / you-losing from the round payloads; exactly one slot gets a gold ribbon + bounce — `STRIKE NOW` on cleave when the foe is staggered, `INTERRUPT` on bash/kick for an interruptible cast, `FINISH` under 22%, red `FLEE` when you are under 25% and behind — and burst/stun skills off cooldown wear a thin gold "ready" rim. The target frame blinks white on your hit and red on theirs. The `FINISH THEM — R` chip moved from 30% height (it covered the tell pill) to just above the bar. (`ui.js`)
- **Less prose over the arena.** Per-swing feed lines ("You hit X", "X hit YOU for 4", "You miss") are dropped while damage numbers are on (they echoed the numbers and stacked three pills over the action every round); telegraph, stagger, loot and death lines remain. Note for the lead: `page.screenshot()` takes ~6-7 s in this environment, so the "1.5 s" storyboard frames are really ~8 s apart and span ~60 s of fight; the kill lands around frame 5-6.
