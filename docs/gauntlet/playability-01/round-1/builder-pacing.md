# Builder: pacing (playability-01, round 1)

Files: `src/combat.py`, `src/mob_ai.py`, `src/config.py` (no mob/zone data touched).

- Root cause in the round-1 evidence: the level-30 warrior swung a bare 1d8 for 2-8 damage into 370-800 HP mobs while eating 15-18 per round plus 25% random mob crits, so both fights were unwinnable slogs that ended in player deaths. Added level-scaled "hero" rules in `combat.py` (constants in `config.py`): players add `level//3` to hit, weapon dice scale `x(1 + 0.22*level) + 0.6*level`, and NPC damage to players is reduced by `1.85%/level` (cap 60%). Mob random crits are pinned to 5% so mob burst comes only from telegraphed wind-ups; an unbraced crushing blow stuns 15% of the time (was 25%, which alone cost 3 rounds in one scratch fight).
- Boss fights cannot be one-shot: a single player swing is capped at 10% of a boss's max HP (`cap_boss_swing`), so Grimclaw lasts >= 10 hits (~11 rounds) instead of dying in 30 s once player damage is fixed.
- Telegraph cadence (`mob_ai._choose_bruiser_intent`): the opening wind-up is guaranteed on the mob's first declare (lands at t<=4 s), then 75%/round after a 10-14 s cooldown, i.e. roughly every 3-4 rounds. Three phrasings each for crushing blow and sweep so the warning line does not repeat verbatim.
- Heavy blows are now measured in "full average hits" (`_full_hit` = dice + the mob's damage bonus): crushing blow = 2.2x, sweep = 1.7x. Before, the heavy used bare dice + damroll and hit for the same 16-18 as a plain swing, so bracing/sidestepping was pointless. A mob mid wind-up swings at half strength that round.
- Staggers are reachable: the stagger budget is primed at `3 + level//8` poise (was `3 + level//4`, 11-12 hits for these mobs), and crits or hits >= 8% of the mob's max HP chip double poise. Hitting a staggered mob prints "You press the opening while X reels — wide open!" so the exploit is visible in the transcript.
- Hit lines are grammatical and rotate: "Your slash wounds X! [22 damage]" / "Your slash lands squarely and ... " / "Your slash connects — it ..." on the player side, and "X's painful blow hits you!" / "X's strike hits you — it nicks you!" / "X's swing hits home, a quick one!" on the receiving side, with a round-robin template index per character so no identical hit line appears two rounds running. Bonus/off-hand attack lines got the same fix. Old form was "Your barely scratch slashes X".
- NPC kills now announce "☠ X is DEAD! You killed X." to the killer and "X is DEAD!" to the room from `handle_death` (the old path only removed the mob silently; the fight harness could not see the kill and logged "timeout"). The low-HP warning is owned by combat: exactly one "Your health is low!" per fight (reset in `start_combat`/`end_combat`), and the once-per-session tip is suppressed so it cannot double up.
- Scratch evidence, final tuning, fresh server (`docs/gauntlet/scratch/round-1/fight/`): grave keeper killed in 46.9 s (12 rounds, first telegraph at ~2.4 s, 12 telegraph / 4 stagger regex hits, player ends 134/207); Grimclaw killed in 43.6 s (11 rounds, 10 telegraph / 4 stagger hits, player ends 138/207). Earlier tunings ran 62-87 s on the keeper, hence the extra hit bonus and flat damage. Caveat for the critic run: `tools/gauntlet/capture.js` and the smoke suite log in as the same `Gauntlet` character; when another login overlaps a fight the second login replaces the player object and the transcript stops mid-way (seen three times in scratch runs, as "Gauntlet has entered the realm" mid-fight) — run fight.py alone on a freshly restarted server.
