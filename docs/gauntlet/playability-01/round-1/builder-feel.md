# playability-01 / round 1 — builder: feel (combat readability, feedback, rhythm)

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer/scene-topdown.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/ui-arpg.js` (fx-abilities.js untouched).

- **One tell instead of three stacked ones.** The enemy wind-up used to draw a `!`, a separate `⚠ Crushing Blow` caption, and a HUD line, all overlapping the mob name and the damage numbers. The mob now carries a single named pill above its head (`! CRUSHING BLOW`) whose bottom edge is the strike timer filling toward the moment it lands (turns gold in the last 30%); the contracting ring, the reticle on your feet and the intent beam are unchanged. (`scene-topdown.js` startWindup / tickWindup / syncIntentBanners)
- **Damage numbers get lanes.** Pops near the same anchor within 0.9s climb a lane each instead of smearing into "-15 4 -30". Your hits pop on the mob's far side; hits you take pop on your far side, in bold ember red one size up, so the two columns never share a spot. Stroke went 2→3/4 with a drop shadow so numbers read over the pale crypt floor; the 5–12 tier moved from green (vanished on the haze) to warm white/gold. (`damageNumber`, `fxHit`, `fxMiss`, `fxTaken`, `dmgStyle`)
- **Who is winning, on the target frame.** A YOU-vs-FOE meter under the enemy HP bar: your bar grows left from a centre line, theirs grows right, with a one-word tag (`▲ WINNING` / `◆ EVEN` / `▼ LOSING`, the last one pulsing). Shown only while in combat with a non-friendly target. (`ui.js` updateDuelMeter, injected `#feel-css`)
- **Who is winning, in the world.** In combat your own HP bar floats over your head in the same green→amber→red language as the mob's bar, with a blue rim to say "you" and a red beating rim under 25%, so the two bars can be compared in one glance. (`scene-topdown.js` drawPlayerHpBar, drawn above the haze layers)
- **The HUD wind-up plate says when.** `⚠ INCOMING · CRUSHING BLOW · 2.3s` with a live 100ms countdown that turns red under 1s and reads `NOW` at zero; the plate glows on a 0.55s pulse. (`ui.js` updateIntentUI / tickWindupCd)
- **Reaction prompt as an invitation.** Chips are restyled as key-cap buttons under a `REACT` label; chips that do not apply to this tell are hidden rather than dimmed (two buttons, not three with one greyed), and the answer that fits the tell (interrupt a cast, sidestep a sweep, brace a heavy blow) bounces with a gold rim. Hotkeys/click behaviour unchanged. (`ui.js` CSS + `best` class)
- **HP bar ghost on the dock.** When HP drops, a pale ghost of the lost chunk hangs on the big HP bar for 0.3s then drains, so the size of the hit reads without reading the number. (`ui-arpg.js` map handler; CSS in `ui.js`)
- Not changed: hit-stop, knockback, flashes, camera shake, slash arcs and the STAGGERED / DODGE / BRACED words already existed and are kept as-is; the whitewashed crypt lighting is outside this piece.
