# playability-02 / round 1 — builder: feel (combat readability, feedback, rhythm)

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer/scene-topdown.js`, `fx-abilities.js`, `ui.js` (`ui-arpg.js` unchanged — it already flashes the dock HP bar and paints a ghost of lost HP). Built on top of the uncommitted round-2/3 feel code from playability-01 (bracket, ledger, reels, reaction strip). Verified with three scratch captures of the `fight` label (scratchpad only; no harness file changed).

- **Paired HP bars in the arena, sized for the storyboard shrink.** The engaged foe's head bar goes 32x3.5 -> 44x5 with a red rim and 25% ticks; your own over-head bar goes 24x3 -> 36x5 with the blue rim and the same ticks. Two bars, two rim colours, the longer one is winning — readable after the 1280 -> ~500 px montage shrink where the old bars were 12 px slivers. (`drawHpBar`, `drawPlayerHpBar`)
- **The enemy's number is no longer a speck next to yours.** Damage ladder low steps +2 pt (9/9/10/11 -> 10/11/12/13) and the number you take is never below the bold/crit threshold (min 12), so `-7` reads at the same weight as `26`. (`dmgStyle`, `fxTaken`)
- **Telegraph: bigger pill, and a wedge of intent.** The `! CRUSHING BLOW` pill is 11 px (was 9) on a 22 px plate, and the wind-up now fills a translucent cone from the attacker's feet onto your reticle that darkens as the timer runs — the direction of the coming blow reads even when the two bodies overlap (the old 1.5 px beam vanished between them). (`startWindup`, `tickWindup`)
- **Impact scales with the blow.** `MH.abilityFx.impact` takes a size (1 -> 2.4 by damage): the star's arms grow and thicken and a soft filled disc appears behind heavy hits, on both the foe (white) and you (rose). A 26 is visibly a bigger picture than a 4 before the number lands. (`fx-abilities.js impact`, `fxHit`, `fxTaken`)
- **The red damage wash no longer eats the frame.** The vignette pulse decays on the wall clock in `update()` (`tickDmgPulse`) and peaks at 0.3 (was 0.6 on the tween clock) — a scratch frame at 1-3 fps had caught it as a solid red screen over the whole fight and HUD. (`dmgPulse`)
- **After the kill the plate says what is next.** On `mob.death` the scene counts the other live hostiles in the room and emits `feel.next`; the `YOU KILLED X` plate grows a second line: `▶ NEXT FOE: <name> [TAB] TARGET · [1] ATTACK` or `▶ CLEAR — CLICK [▼ LOOT] ON THE BODY, THEN MOVE ON`. A bobbing clickable `▼ LOOT` tag also hangs over the fresh body until it is looted, a real new fight starts, or the room changes. (`fxMobDeath`, `showLootTag`, `ui.js nextLine`)
- **The result plate survives the post-kill frames.** It was cleared by `combat.state(on)`, which flickers on from late hit lines after the kill (a scratch capture lost the plate 1 s after the death); it now clears only on a round push with a live mob fighting you, 2.5 s+ after the kill. (`ui.js`)
- **Reaction strip up a size.** `REACT NOW` 10 -> 11 px, chip text 12.5 -> 14 px, keycaps 12 -> 14 px; still above the action bar, over chrome, not arena.
