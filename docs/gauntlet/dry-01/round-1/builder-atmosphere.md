# dry-01 / round 1 — builder: atmosphere

Files: `src/web_isometric/platformer/painter.js`, `src/web_isometric/platformer/themes-zones.js` (immersion.js untouched: nothing in it affects the judged labels).

- Re-palettised every zone theme toward saturated, biome-specific ground (BrowserQuest reads at a glance because its ground is vivid, ours was uniformly dark and grey): forest/elven/meadow are now proper greens, Midgaard is warm sandstone with warm brick walls, the necropolis is bone-white with a cold blue mood, caves/mines are warm dark rock, the sunken coast is turquoise over sand.
- Added `MH.SECTOR_ATMOS` + `MH.roomPalette`: a room's sector can overrule its zone look, so Bear's Den (cave sector inside the forest zone) now paints as a dark cave instead of green grass, and any swimmable sector paints as water regardless of zone.
- Every theme carries a `light` spec; the painter bakes illumination into the ground because the scene's light layer is off in daytime: enclosed biomes get a dark vignette with corners falling to near-black, warm pools under each wall torch (the scene's torch RNG walk is replayed exactly so pools sit under the real torches), pale daylight spilling in at exits, and a hearth pool at the centre so actors stand in the brightest spot.
- Open-sky biomes get sun shadows thrown down-right by every treeline/wall block and dappled canopy sun-spots (additive), plus a soft edge vignette, so the floor is no longer one flat brightness.
- Border-ring sprites are now painted seamless (forms centred on tile edges): treelines, rock faces, running-bond brick walls, ossuary bone walls and coral reefs join into continuous masses instead of one stamp per cell. Organic interior obstacles (the biome-shaped treelines roomgen grows into the room) use the same seamless mass; architectural obstacles stay free-standing blocks.
- New floor kinds in the painter: `bone` (long-bone mosaic with sparse skulls under a cold cast), `shallows` (sand ripples under a turquoise water wash that deepens toward the walls, with caustic light-nets and surface glints), plus snow/runic/brass/checker which previously fell through to bare wash; cobbles are now irregular in size, rotation and tone so no row lines up.
- Water pools: depth-graded colour (pale sandy shallows near shore, deeper blue away), brushed soft edges, a wet-sand rim outside the foam line, caustics in the shallows.
- Verified in headless Chromium captures of the five labels that the painter runs (no fallback to tiles) and the new ring/obstacle textures load; a faint cell-pitch grid visible over open floor predates this round and originates outside these files.
