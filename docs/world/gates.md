# Gatekeepers, zone by zone

Every zone with a boss, and whether a gatekeeper holds the key to the way in (src/rites.py, world/rites.json; see [rites.md](rites.md)). A gate is locked and warded; its keeper, a mini-boss before it, carries the key; the key opens the gate once and crumbles; the zone reset locks it again. Where no door stood on a single way into the boss room, the gate is built there (a gate indoors, a barrier under the sky).

59 of 106 boss zones are gated. Each other row says why not.
