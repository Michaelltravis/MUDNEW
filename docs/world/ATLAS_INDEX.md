# Atlas index: the world on the Bedlam atlas

Generated by `python3 tools/atlas/match.py && python3 tools/atlas/index.py` from `world/atlas/bedlam.json` (the owner's atlas), our zones and `world/atlas/overrides.json`. Edit the overrides, not this page. The full room-by-room lists are in `world/atlas/index.json`.

## At a glance

- **The atlas:** 8,038 rooms in 91 zones, joined by 268 cross-zone exits.
- **Matched to ours:** 18 zones are rooms we already have; 9 are like a zone of ours (the atlas layout replaces ours, our creatures and items move onto it); 64 are new.
- **Open:** 36 zones (the matched and similar ones, the connectors and the city wall); the other 55 stay sealed until they are built.
- **Our 32 zones that are not on the atlas** keep their content and join it through a connector (see Hookups).
- **Still to write:** descriptions for 6,753 rooms; 186 borrowed descriptions to check (ours, written for a room the atlas names differently).
- **Exits:** 389 of our rooms have exits that differ from the atlas (the atlas wins); the atlas has 672 one-way exits and 154 rooms with no exits.
- **Water:** 558 water rooms will need a boat or raft; 11 places in our world give a boat today.

## For the owner to review before the world is re-laid

1. **Hookups:** where each of our zones joins the atlas (below). Move any by adding it to `hookups` in `world/atlas/overrides.json`.
2. **Similar zones:** our zone each one replaces (below). Wrong pairs: edit `similar`.
3. **Our rooms the atlas lacks:** kept as an annex, their creatures moved, or dropped (below).
4. **Connectors:** the zones that open now with placeholder descriptions: edit `connectors`.
